Poems

Pothole pilgrim
mumbled over and over in '25

Pothole pilgrim,
how fares the rain?

Make way for my 
bigger car!


Agues Calientes butterfly sanctuary
@ the foot of Machu Picchu, just after Christmas '25
How far beneath
tender soil must I
bury the stench of 
my loss?

How many times
must compost be 
my oldest friend?

Is fortune not a box
of butterflies
stuck
beneath Incan sights?

Incan dreams on
mountain tops
are only dreams for me.

Is it meant to
take this long
between a 
state of mind?

Flapping wings I
grew myself
vestigial
in the box
of butterflies.

Beneath sky and stone
temple, tourist, touch of guilt:

Box of butterflies.


Medicine or mummy
written in an air-conned Amazonian sanctuary, late '25

Are they lost or settled?
am I lost or settled?

How to phrase the
language gap
when neither seems
inclined to speak on yesteryear
on yesterday
on 25 or 46
on glazy eyes
on poncho rain
on dreaded locks
on patchy teeth
on medicine in this & that
on anything but liquor

No, thanks is universal
a smile too

Groan,
a Western nicety that warns of
glaciers melting 
in the mouths of travelling jesters
each a story larger than last

Each a family waiting for a 
Christmas cracker
to crack the lonely sight of the 
empty chair

‘Next year’
they fib
‘Next year’
they beg

Empty chair
or fireworks
the choice was
made before
the medicine
releases grip

Tristan! Time
for more!
Meditate the medicine
find the rivers pull
pray the current’s tender
touch
leaves bloodstains 
leaves pools

Contemplate further thoughts
yell sod it
in a hundred tongues
filtered 
through the wax of
unwashed ears
glazy eyes
medicine & more
medicine
grabbing above the cot
medicine
murmur it to a bare breast
medicine

Drink from the 
desire
to crawl in
reverse step

Slipping beneath that
bloody flow of medicine
and time

In medicine
and medicine

Until the empty chair
is full 
and you gurgle and cry and jest and 
clap your mucky
baby hands on your
hand-washed baby bib and preach
out for medicine
and for mummy.

Ought we turn?
read a few times on the balcony of a party, May ’25

I am mad certain
we have gone the
wrong way!

I simply do not possess the
wits & whatnots to 
appropriately express
the message so instead
here I go, I spit forward 
onto familiar
recipients which is frustrating — in its own
way — for all involved
parties.

& now friends
having listened so
politely
are bifurcated; split down two;
chopped wood;
weighing the momentary
contemplations for I
look a little boy in
a passing bought of stage fright 
which is frankly 
wrong for right now I am
King!

& then a beat more
pushes on
Lime bike rattles by &
this
sweat stained copper crown
rusts into
bark & blood

& now I am mad certain
it’ll rot &
pool in the corner
of an unkempt bedroom
near other hindsight &
regret & an open drawer.
